# Plugin Store promo images

Marketing images for the Book listing on the Craft Plugin Store, rendered in the same theme as the
plugin's marketing page at
[justinholt.com/plugins/craft-book](https://justinholt.com/plugins/craft-book).

## Building

```bash
./build.sh          # all slides
./build.sh "2 5"    # just slides 2 and 5
```

Output lands in `out/` as `book-promo-N.png`, 1920×1080 (rendered at 2× in headless Chrome, then
downsampled so the type stays crisp).

## Slides

| # | Slide | Shows |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | Cover — name, tagline, app icon, free badge | — |
| 2 | `Download our brochure (PDF, 4.2 MB)` → `{book:brochure:render}` | the whole pitch, in one line |
| 3 | The five viewers, and who renders each | that `auto` is a resolution, not a viewer |
| 4 | A verdict: one chosen, three ruled out with reasons | the distinguishing idea |
| 5 | Publish the whole volume → a signed route per document | private assets without a public volume |
| 6 | A CSV rendered as a table by Craft itself | the formats that need no viewer |
| 7 | `craft.book.embed()` and `resolve()` in a template | the Twig API |

## Notes

Book is **free with no editions**, so like Bed and Blaster and unlike the paid plugins in the
family there is no price on the cover badge and no figure anywhere in the deck that a pricing
change could strand.

The **watermark is an outline, and the icon's tile is stripped** — the two corrections every deck
in this family needs. The tile goes because at slide scale its edge is a hard rectangle laid over
the artwork. The fill goes because Book's mark is two large solid slabs: at 5% opacity they read
as two grey rectangles, and the fold between them, which is the only thing saying *book* rather
than *panels*, disappears completely. Stroked, both pages and the fold survive.

**The watermark had to be moved.** At `left: -6%` — Bed's crop — the left page was sliced off at
x=0 and the fold floated with nothing to its left, legible as *something*, not as a book. Book's
mark is wide and short, so it wants a shallower crop than a tall icon: `left: -1%`, `top: 5%`,
1150px puts the whole thing on the slide.

Three layout rules inherited from Blaster's deck, and all three bit again:

- **One line per bullet, about 55 characters at 25px.** The copy column is 780px inside a 130px
  gutter and the panel starts at `left: 900px`, so a bullet that wraps runs at the panel and lands
  on top of it — nothing clips, the text simply overlaps. Four of the eighteen bullets had to be
  cut back after the first render, and 55 is a safer ceiling here than Blaster's 58 because Book's
  bullets carry more monospace, which is wider.
- **`.points li` is a flex container.** An inline `<span class="mono">` left loose in the text
  becomes a *sibling flex item* and picks up the 16px gap on both sides. Every point keeps its text
  in one `<span class="t">`.
- **A dashed 1px border does not read at 1920.** Slide 3 has to distinguish the two viewers a third
  party renders from the three the site renders itself, and that distinction is carried by *colour*
  on the state label — parchment for Microsoft and Google, sage for the rest — never by a border
  style that vanishes on downsampling.

Slide 7 is a code card rather than a terminal, because Book ships no console commands. The card
uses the same geometry as Bed's `.term` (`left: 900px; right: -110px`, bleeding off the right
edge), with a filename bar instead of traffic lights.

`fonts.css` is generated by `build.sh`, which base64-inlines the two woff2 files in `assets/` —
Chrome's `file://` origin rules block them otherwise. It is gitignored; don't edit it.

`assets/icon.svg` is a copy of `src/icon.svg`, and `assets/watermark.svg` carries the same two
`rect` elements under the same `skewY()` transforms. Three files, one geometry — change the icon
and change all three.
